# gold_spatial_soil

## Scientific scenario

Synthetic soil-property observations over a regular spatial grid. The response contains a broad deterministic trend and smooth spatial structure. The dataset validates coordinate declarations and prepares the spatial-validation contracts introduced in version 0.2.0.

## Validation target

Coordinates `x` and `y` must remain associated with the design specification and portable manifests. Random row-wise validation should not be interpreted as evidence of geographic transferability.

## Pedagogical trap

Nearby locations contain related information. Randomly mixing neighbors between train and test can overstate out-of-location predictive performance.
